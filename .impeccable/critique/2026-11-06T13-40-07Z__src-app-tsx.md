#### Overall Impression
WAYLINE possesses a distinct, tactile personality that stands out from generic software. However, in life-critical scenarios, its engineering-first disclosure and cognitive panic triggers (specifically the SOS auto-countdown racing against typing) actively undermine operator clarity.

#### What's Working
1. **Defensive GPS Fallback**: Immediate manual landmark input (`MANUAL LANDMARK / FLOOR / ROOM`) prevents infinite spinners when browser geolocation times out indoors or over LAN HTTP.
2. **Tactile Physics**: Physical button states (`translate(2px, 2px)` with solid `#292D29` ink borders) provide unmistakable confirmation under wet or cold finger taps.
3. **Emergency Category Recognition**: Standardized, high-contrast Lucide icons (HeartPulse, AlertOctagon, Waves, LifeBuoy) enable split-second triage recognition.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P0] SOS Auto-Submit Races Against Typing**: The 10s auto-submit timer continues ticking while the user is actively typing critical notes or injuries, dispatching incomplete messages.
  - *Why it matters*: Victims in crisis experience severe tunnel vision; a racing countdown causes panic and premature transmission of empty alerts.
  - *Fix*: Pause auto-submit (`autoSubmitActive = false`) immediately upon user focus or input in description/landmark fields.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable harden src/components/SosModal.tsx`
- **[P1] Mobile Bottom Nav Overcrowded (7 Actions)**: Seven interactive destinations crammed into mobile viewport widths (<390px).
  - *Why it matters*: Violates mobile HIG (max 3–5 items) and causes high mis-tap rates between SOS, Alerts, and Chat.
  - *Fix*: Consolidate to 4 primary tabs (Desk, Messages, Alerts, More) with an elevated center SOS trigger.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable layout src/components/Navigation.tsx`
- **[P2] Leaking Engineering Jargon to End Users**: Raw daemon terms (`UDP_LAN`, `DAEMON ONLINE`, `Listening Address`, `Scan Subnet`) displayed on main user dashboards.
  - *Why it matters*: Intimidates non-technical civilian operators during disaster grid collapse.
  - *Fix*: Translate terms to human operational language: "Mesh Ready", "Search for Nearby Radios", "Your Device Address".
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable clarify src/components/FieldDesk.tsx`
- **[P2] Synchronous `window.prompt()` for Host Configuration**: Clicking "Set Host IP" invokes blocking browser `prompt()`.
  - *Why it matters*: Easily blocked by mobile browsers, freezes UI, and lacks regex IP validation.
  - *Fix*: Replace with an inline modal/card input with formatted IP validation.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable harden src/components/FieldDesk.tsx`

#### Persona Red Flags
- **Tactical Rural Volunteer Responder (Direct Sunlight, Wet Gloves)**:
  - 7-icon mobile bottom bar leads to frequent accidental clicks between SOS and Nearby.
  - Low-contrast dot pattern (`#D6CEBD`) washes out in bright glare.
- **Trapped Civilian First-Timer (Flooded House, No Cell Service)**:
  - Accidentally tapping SOS triggers a flashing `AUTO-TRANSMITTING IN 10s` countdown, inducing panic.
  - Sees status `SAVED_LOCALLY` and incorrectly believes transmission failed because cellular towers are down.
